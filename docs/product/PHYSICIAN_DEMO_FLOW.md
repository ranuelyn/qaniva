# Physician demo flow (2–4 minutes)

The demo's job: show that Qaniva is a coherent clinical-decision simulation
product with deterministic, evidence-referenced scoring — and collect the
physician's clinical review. Say early and plainly: **"the clinical content is
evidence-based, fictional, and awaiting formal physician validation — that
review is exactly what we'd like from you."**

| # | Step | What the presenter says | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Launch → Home | "Qaniva — interactive clinical decision simulation." Point at Continue/Cases/Progress: no accounts, no gimmicks. | 10 s |
| 2 | Cases → STEMI card | "Each case is a versioned, evidence-referenced definition. The card shows the presentation, never the diagnosis." | 10 s |
| 3 | Briefing | "A structured prebrief: role, setting, resources, the triage note, your task. All authored case data." | 15 s |
| 4 | Enter simulation | "Full-screen 3D patient. The five areas — Patient, Examine, Orders, Treat, More — and everything you see (vitals, results, availability) comes from a deterministic engine." | 20 s |
| 5 | Take history → attach monitor → **order the ECG** | Open the tracing: "the learner reads it themselves — and note the provenance line: this tracing is a schematic placeholder pending clinical verification." (Say it aloud; do not skip.) | 30 s |
| 6 | Aspirin → IV → ticagrelor → heparin → **activate the cath lab** | "Timing matters: the same actions later score differently — and if the activation is late enough, the patient actually deteriorates." | 40 s |
| 7 | Statin → handoff → Results | "Case complete, 88 points. Look at the sections: on-time with timestamps, harmful separated from merely unnecessary, accepted alternatives — ticagrelor or prasugrel score identically." | 30 s |
| 8 | Scroll: causality + evidence + timeline | "Every line traces to the rubric and to evidence IDs from the case's evidence ledger. Nothing here is generated by an LLM." | 20 s |
| 9 | Back → Anaphylaxis card | "Second case, same engine, zero new code — IM epinephrine timing, route safety, biphasic-aware disposition." | 10 s |
| 10 | Progress tab | "Attempts persist locally; replay is one tap. That's the loop: brief, decide, debrief, repeat." | 10 s |

**Do not overclaim:** no "clinically approved/validated", no learning-outcome
claims, no "AI-powered" framing (the AI never controls the simulation), no
device claims (simulator build). **Do say:** deterministic and replayable ·
evidence-referenced · timing/order/state-aware · clinical validation pending —
"we want your red pen" (`docs/clinical/cases/*/REVIEW.md`).

Where validation-pending must be mentioned: step 5 (ECG placeholder), step 8
(evidence), and closing. It is also stated in-product under
Settings → Educational use & clinical status.
